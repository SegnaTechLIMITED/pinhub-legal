PinHub · invited beta

Privacy Notice

Effective 17 November 2026 · Version Beta 1.1

This notice describes the current PinHub.golf mobile beta distributed through Apple TestFlight and, when enabled, Google Play internal testing. SegnaTech LIMITED operates the beta. For privacy questions or requests, email admin@segnatech.co.uk.

What the beta does

Invited testers can explore pilot golf courses, record rounds on their device, view live player-to-green distance where course coordinates are available, and preview the Iford FootGolf league concept. FlingGolf, FootGolf and SpeedGolf are shown. The league preview does not take real bookings, registrations or payments. DiscGolf and Player Connections, including nearby-player discovery and chat, are switched off in the current build.

Information we handle

  • Invited account: email address, account identifier, sign-in and password-reset information, and any profile details supplied for the test. Supabase provides authentication; Brevo delivers authentication email.
  • Course use: authenticated requests to the PinHub API for pilot course data. Railway hosts the API and may process standard connection and error logs. GolfAPI.io supplies course data to the server; your precise device position is not sent to GolfAPI.io for the distance feature.
  • Location: when you open a distance or round feature and allow foreground location, the app uses your device position to calculate distance to a course point. The current distance feature does not send your precise position to PinHub servers. Your device's operating-system location services also process location under your device settings.
  • Local information: round scores, score history, provisional handicap calculations, settings and cached course information are stored on the test device. The current beta does not sync ordinary round scores to a PinHub account.
  • Testing and support: Apple or Google may provide tester and diagnostic information. We process feedback, screenshots and messages you choose to send to the support address.

We do not use this beta for targeted advertising or sell personal data. We do not intentionally collect payment-card information or special-category information through the current app. Please do not put sensitive information about yourself or another person into test feedback.

Why we use it

We use account information to provide and protect the invited beta, course requests to show the pilot layouts, and optional foreground location to calculate distances. We use technical logs and feedback to investigate defects and keep the service secure. Our UK GDPR bases are legitimate interests in operating and improving a limited beta, performance of the testing arrangement where applicable, and consent where we ask for it. Location access also requires your device permission, which you can withdraw in settings; non-location features remain available.

Where information goes

Supabase processes invited-account data; Brevo sends account email; Railway hosts the PinHub API; Expo, Apple and Google support build distribution and testing. These providers may process data in different countries under their applicable terms and safeguards. We do not share personal data with venues, governing bodies or advertisers for their own marketing. Access is limited to authorised team members and service providers that need it for the beta.

Retention and deletion

Invited-account information remains while the beta account is active and is removed following a verified deletion request, subject to any necessary security or legal retention. Local scores, round history and settings remain on this device until the app and its local data are removed. The current beta has no in-app round-history deletion control. We normally retain voluntarily supplied feedback and defect evidence for no more than 12 months unless an issue or legal duty requires longer. Apple and Google retain their own distribution records under their policies.

You can request deletion of your PinHub account and associated data from within the app through the privacy contact, or use our account deletion request page. We may verify that the request comes from the account holder. You may also request access, correction, export, restriction or objection through admin@segnatech.co.uk. UK users may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Children, security and changes

The current invited beta is not intended for unsupervised children. Player Connections and chat are disabled. We use encrypted connections and controlled account access, though no beta can be guaranteed risk-free. Report a security or privacy concern to admin@segnatech.co.uk.

We will update this notice and the app's disclosures before enabling new features such as social discovery, messaging, online competitions or payments.
