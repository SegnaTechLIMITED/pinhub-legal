Beta Privacy Notice

Effective 3 September 2026 · Version Beta 1.0

This notice explains how personal data is handled when invited testers use the PinHub mobile-app prototype through Apple TestFlight or a Google Play testing track.

Who is responsible

PinHub is operated for beta testing by SegnaTech LIMITED. For privacy questions, requests or complaints, email admin@segnatech.co.uk.

Beta scope

The beta is a functional prototype for FlingGolf, FootGolf, DiscGolf and SpeedGolf. It is not a production service. Payments, public social discovery and online competition functions are disabled. Testers must not enter sensitive personal information, confidential venue information or information about another person.

Data the current beta may handle

  • TestFlight or Google Play tester and diagnostic information made available by Apple or Google.
  • Feedback, screenshots and communications that a tester chooses to send.
  • A demonstration profile, sport preference, settings, locally recorded rounds, scores and provisional handicap data stored on the test device.
  • Precise device location while the tester actively uses a round feature and grants permission. The app displays accuracy information; the prototype does not treat device location as a surveyed course measurement.
  • Basic technical logs needed to diagnose a reported fault if a connected beta service is enabled.

The current prototype does not intentionally collect payment-card details, advertising identifiers or special-category data.

Why data is used and legal basis

PurposeUK GDPR basis
Provide the invited beta and its core functionsLegitimate interests in testing and improving PinHub; performance of an agreed beta where applicable
Use device location during a roundConsent through the device permission and the tester's choice to use the feature
Diagnose crashes, security issues and defectsLegitimate interests in operating a safe and reliable beta
Respond to feedback or privacy requestsLegitimate interests and legal obligation where applicable

Location permission may be refused or withdrawn in device settings. The tester can still use non-location parts of the prototype.

Storage, sharing and international processing

Prototype profile, preference and round data is currently designed to remain on the device unless a connected test service is explicitly enabled. Apple processes TestFlight information under Apple's terms; Google processes Play testing information under Google's terms. Approved development or hosting suppliers may process the minimum data needed to operate or diagnose the beta under confidentiality and data-protection obligations.

PinHub does not sell personal data. Personal data is not shared with venues, governing bodies or advertisers for their own marketing. If a supplier processes data outside the UK, an appropriate transfer safeguard will be used where required.

Retention and deletion

  • On-device prototype data remains until the tester clears it, deletes it using an available app control, or removes the app.
  • Tester feedback and defect evidence is kept only as long as needed to resolve the issue and document the beta, normally no more than 12 months unless a legal or security reason requires longer.
  • Distribution-platform records are retained by Apple or Google under their own policies.

To request access, correction, export, restriction, objection or deletion, email admin@segnatech.co.uk. The request may need identity verification. UK users may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children and safeguarding

The external beta is not intended for unsupervised use by children. A tester under 18 must participate only through an approved, supervised programme with appropriate parental or guardian arrangements. Do not use beta feedback channels to disclose safeguarding details; contact the responsible safeguarding lead or emergency services where appropriate.

Security and changes

Access is limited to invited testers and authorised team members. Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are used, but no prototype can be guaranteed risk-free. Report suspected security or privacy incidents promptly to admin@segnatech.co.uk.

This notice will be updated before production launch and whenever the beta adds accounts, cloud storage, analytics, payments, social features or new data providers. Material changes will be communicated to testers.
