Request account deletion

To delete your invited PinHub.golf beta account, email admin@segnatech.co.uk with the subject “PinHub account deletion request”. Send the request from the email address used for your PinHub account if possible. Do not include your password, verification code or other sensitive information.

We may verify that you control the account before removing it. We will delete the account and associated account data, subject to information we must retain for a specific security or legal reason. We will explain any such retention in our reply. Ordinary round scores and settings are stored on your device; remove the app or clear its local data to delete those copies.

You can also start this request from the privacy contact in the app. Read the PinHub.golf privacy notice for more about data handling and your rights.
